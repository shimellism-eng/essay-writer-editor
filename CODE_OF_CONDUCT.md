# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in Essay Writer & Editor a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, colour, religion, sexual identity and orientation, or writing background.

We aim to create a community in which people can discuss writing, education and AI without shaming students, multilingual writers, non-native writers, people who use assistive technology or anyone whose prose does not match a narrow idea of “normal.”

## Expected behaviour

Examples of behaviour that helps the community include:

- showing empathy and respect;
- giving specific criticism of ideas or tool behaviour rather than attacking a person;
- welcoming questions from nontechnical contributors;
- respecting dialect, code-switching and different writing traditions;
- acknowledging uncertainty and correcting mistakes openly;
- protecting the privacy of writers whose work is discussed;
- accepting responsibility and learning from feedback.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Examples of unacceptable behaviour include:

- harassment, threats, insults or discriminatory comments;
- sexualised language or unwanted sexual attention;
- deliberate misgendering or use of rejected names;
- publishing another person's private information without permission;
- submitting private student work or identifying a suspected writer;
- using an AI-writing assessment to publicly accuse, shame or target a person;
- sustained disruption, trolling or bad-faith argument;
- retaliation against someone who reports a concern;
- conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in repository spaces and when a person is publicly representing the project, including issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews and project-related events.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. They may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behaviour is inappropriate, threatening, offensive or harmful.

Do not include sensitive personal information in a public issue. For threats, harassment or other GitHub policy violations, use [GitHub's private abuse-reporting process](https://support.github.com/contact/report-abuse). The project does not currently publish a separate private conduct mailbox, so it must not promise a confidential project channel that does not exist.

Maintainers will respect the privacy and safety of a reporter as far as reasonably possible. A maintainer who has a conflict of interest should not handle the report.

## Enforcement guidelines

Maintainers may apply the following responses according to the impact and context of the behaviour.

### 1. Correction

A private or public explanation of why behaviour was inappropriate, with a request to correct it.

### 2. Warning

A warning that continued behaviour will lead to restrictions. The warning may include conditions on further participation.

### 3. Temporary restriction

A temporary ban from interaction with the project, including unsolicited contact with people involved in the report.

### 4. Permanent restriction

A permanent ban when there is a pattern of violations or a serious incident involving harassment, threats, discrimination or harm.
